Field notes

Text format version 1

Set up the sheet

plan v1
title "Garden studio"
units cm
scale 1:50
sheet A3 landscape

Draw structure

wall north from 0,0 to 600,0 thickness 15
door entry on south at 220 width 90 swing left
window view on north at 180 width 160

Add notes and dimensions

label "Living room" at 300,220 size 18
dimension from 0,0 to 600,0 offset -55
# this line is a note

Door and window positions start at the wall’s first point.

A positive dimension offset sits left of its start-to-end direction.

Units: mm, cm, m, in, ft. Paper: A4, A3, A2, Letter, Tabloid.
