Plain-language policy

Privacy

Your plan stays under your control.

Effective 28 August 2026

Your floor plan is yours. The editor does not send plan text, drawings, filenames, or clipboard contents to us.

What this browser stores

Your latest plan text stays in this browser so it survives a refresh. The offline app files may stay in its cache.

Demo changes use a separate demo: storage area. Leaving the demo removes them without reading or changing your plan.

What a share link contains

A share link puts the complete plan after the address’s # mark. Browsers do not send that part to a server.

Anyone with the link can read the plan. Treat the link like the source file.

What we do not use

This release has no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, remote fonts, or third-party scripts.

Remove stored data

Use your browser’s site-data controls to remove saved plan text and offline files.
