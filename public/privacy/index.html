Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026

Your floor plan is yours. Floorplan Text runs in your browser and does not send plan text, exported drawings, filenames, or clipboard contents to us.

What is stored

The latest source text is saved in your browser’s local storage so it survives a refresh. The offline app shell may be stored in your browser cache. You can remove both through your browser’s site-data controls.

Sharing

“Copy share link” puts the complete source into the URL after the #. Browsers do not send that fragment to a web server. Anyone you give the link to can read the plan, so treat it like the source file.

Measurement

This version includes no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, accounts, cookies, or third-party runtime services.

Questions

Open an issue in the public source repository.
